The service

Repvo creates workout and nutrition plans automatically from the information you enter, using formulas and programs designed by certified coaches. Optional add-ons are a monthly coach follow-up over WhatsApp and a scheduled call with a coach.

Plans are general fitness and nutrition guidance. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise or eating plan, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication. If you tell us about certain health conditions, we don't generate an automatic plan and a coach builds it with you on a call instead.

You must be 18 or older to buy a plan on your own. Clients under 18 are handled through a coach call with a parent or guardian present.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in US dollars. Card payments are processed securely by our payment provider; we never see or store your card number. If your card is charged in Egyptian pounds, the amount is converted at the rate shown at checkout.

Coach follow-up

The coach follow-up is a monthly service. The first month is charged at checkout, and it renews on the same day each month. You can stop at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp or replying to any renewal email; it stops at the end of the month you've paid for.

Refunds and cancellations

Plans are digital and delivered instantly, so plan purchases are non-refundable once the plan has been opened, except where a technical problem on our side stopped you from receiving it. Coach calls can be moved or cancelled for a full refund up to 24 hours before the call. Contact us at {{BUSINESS_EMAIL}} within 14 days of a charge if you believe it was made in error.

Privacy

We collect what you enter in the form (body stats, goal, training preferences, foods you avoid, health answers) and your contact details. We use them only to build your plan, deliver it by email, and let a coach contact you on WhatsApp. Your plan link is private: anyone with the link can see the plan, so don't share it publicly. We don't sell your data. You can delete your account and data yourself at any time: sign in at My plans (on the website or in the app), open "Delete my account" and confirm. This removes your name, contact details, health answers, plans and logs and stops any coach follow-up; payment amounts and dates are kept for our accounting. You can also email {{BUSINESS_EMAIL}}.

Sign-in. When you sign in we email you a one-time code and keep you signed in on that device for 90 days with a cookie. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Our apps. The Repvo apps for iPhone and Android show the same account and plans as the website. They don't track you across other apps or websites. Plans you've opened are kept on your device so you can read them offline.
