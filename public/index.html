How your plan is built

No guesswork and no copied templates. These are the methods behind every number you get.

Calories from proven formulas

Mifflin-St Jeor from your weight, height and age; Katch-McArdle when you know your body fat; or your measured InBody BMR.

Protein for your goal

1.8 to 2.2 g per kg depending on your goal, fat of at least 0.7 g per kg, and carbs filling the rest. Every meal solved in exact grams.

Training that fits your week

A split for 2 to 6 days, 4 sets per exercise, reps and rest set for your goal, and a 4-week progression using reps in reserve.

Safety first

A health check sends medical cases to a coach instead of an automatic plan, and injuries swap out the exercises that load them.

Cardio from your heart rate

Heart-rate zones from your age, sessions placed around your lifting days, and a daily step goal that builds over 4 weeks.

People behind the plan

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is this safe if I have a medical condition?

If you tell us about a heart condition, pregnancy, diabetes, kidney or liver disease, or a recent injury, we don't auto-generate a plan. You book a call and a coach builds it with you.

I don't have an InBody scan. Can I still use it?

Yes. Weight, height and age are enough. If you add body fat % or BMR from a scan, your calories are calculated from your lean mass instead.

What if I don't like some foods?

Remove them before checkout and they never appear in your plan. After you get the plan, you can still swap any food and the grams adjust.

Can I get my plan in Arabic?

Yes. Choose العربية as the program language in the form. Your plan, food names, exercises, recipes, trackers and emails all come in Arabic.

How do I receive my plan?

It opens on screen right after payment and is sent to your email. Coach messages come on WhatsApp.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Choose home with dumbbells or bodyweight only, and every exercise is picked for that setup.

Can I get a refund?

Your plan is delivered instantly, so plan purchases are final once you have opened it. If something went wrong with your order, or you need to move or cancel a coach call at least 24 hours before it starts, message us on WhatsApp and we will sort it out. Full details are in our terms.

Why is it so cheap?

The plan is calculated automatically from coach-designed formulas and programs, so we don't charge you for hours of manual work. You pay for a coach's time only if you want it.
